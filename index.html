Book list

The 16 titles and authors come from the 2026–2027 OBOB 3rd–5th Grade Division list that was given to us when we built this app.

Questions and answers

The built-in questions were written from general knowledge of each book. They were not checked page by page against the books, so a few details could be wrong. If a question doesn't match your copy, trust the book.

  • Built-in questions: Dogtown, Frindle, The Lightning Thief, Esperanza Rising, Caterpillar Summer, Connect the Stars, A Kind of Spark, The Magic Misfits, The Misadventures of the Family Fletcher, Snow and Rose, The Witch Boy.
  • Sample cards only (no story details yet): The Grace of Wild Things, The Guardian Test, It's the End of the World and I'm in My Bathing Suit, Light and Air, Murder at the Museum. These only use each book's title and author.
  • Questions marked "My question" were added on this device by you.

Scoring and timing

The default points (5 for a full answer, 3 for a partial one) and the 15-second answer time are based on how OBOB battles usually run. Rules can change from year to year and region to region, so check with your coach. You can change the points under "Points per answer".
