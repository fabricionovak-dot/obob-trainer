Book list

The 16 titles and authors come from the 2026–2027 OBOB 3rd–5th Grade Division list that was given to us when we built this app.

Questions and answers

All 16 books have built-in questions. We wrote every question in our own words and checked the facts against public summaries and reviews (publishers, Kirkus, Common Sense Media, author websites and reading guides). Nothing is copied from the books or from other quiz sites. They were not checked page by page against the books, so a few details could be wrong. If a question doesn't match your copy, trust the book, and tap "Report this question" so we can fix it.

  • Most books have about 24 questions. A few have fewer because there was less public information to check facts against: Connect the Stars, Murder at the Museum, The Guardian Test, It's the End of the World and I'm in My Bathing Suit, Dogtown and The Grace of Wild Things.
  • Questions marked "My question" were added on this device by you.
  • Easy, Medium and Hard levels and the "Where this happens" notes were set by us as a guess. Easy = the main idea of the book, Hard = small details like names of places or minor characters.

Scoring and timing

The default points (5 for a full answer, 3 for a partial one) and the 15-second answer time are based on how OBOB battles usually run. Rules can change from year to year and region to region, so check with your coach. You can change the points under "Points per answer".

How question difficulty works

Each question gets a level based on how big a part of the book it is. The bigger the part, the more likely you remember it after reading the book once.

LevelWhat it asks aboutExamples
Easy The main idea of the book, the thing it's known for. Usually it's on the back cover. Main character names too. "In which book does a boy invent a new word for a pen?"
"Which Greek god is Percy's father?" (Poseidon)
Medium Key events in the middle of the story and important side characters. "What illness does Mama get in California?" (Valley Fever)
"Who stole Zeus's master bolt?" (Luke)
Hard Small details: place names, full names, minor characters, or lists where you need every part. "What is the name of the family's ranch in Mexico?" (El Rancho de las Rosas)
"Name all four Fletcher brothers."

For In Which Book questions, it's about how quickly a moment points to its book. The main idea is Easy. A scene from the middle of the story is Medium or Hard.

These levels are our best guess and haven't been tested with readers. If a question feels easier or harder to you, you're probably right.
